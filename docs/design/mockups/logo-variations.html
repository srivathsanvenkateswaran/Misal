Misal · app icon · round two

Less noodle, more misal

You were right about the shipped one. Five variations that fix the actual cause, not the drawing.

The mistake was the diagnosis, not the execution. Sev was drawn as four long continuous arcs, and long parallel strokes in a bowl are noodles — that is what the shape means before anyone reads the palette. Real sev is fine crisp crumb: short, broken, scattered.

And the palette was all gold. This is the bigger error. A misal is red — the tarri, that fiery slick, is what you recognise from across a room. A gold-only bowl has no misal in it at all. Every variation below leads with rassa red and uses no stroke longer than a crumb.

Misal pav

The pav does the disambiguating that no amount of redrawing the bowl can. A bowl on its own is a bowl of anything; a bowl with a bun beside it is one dish in the world. If the worry is "does this read as misal", this is the variation that answers it outright.

Strata

The dish in cross-section — sev, tarri, rassa, usal, in the order they are assembled. No strokes at all, so no noodles are possible. It is also the only one that is still literally an allocation: separate components, each keeping its character, stacked into one total.
