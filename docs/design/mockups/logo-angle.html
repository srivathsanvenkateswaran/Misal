At the angle you actually meet a bowl

Four marks at the three-quarter angle of your fourth reference — the elliptical steel rim, the body of the bowl falling away beneath it, and the toppings sitting on red.

Why this angle does more work than either flat one. Top-down shows the food and hides the vessel; side-on shows the vessel and hides the food. Three-quarter is the only view that carries both — and the steel katori is doing as much identifying here as the sev is, because a steel bowl with an orange rim of oil is not a dish from anywhere else.

The cost is honest: this is the most detailed thing I have drawn for you, and detail is what dies at 16 px. Judge these on the small rungs, not the large ones.
